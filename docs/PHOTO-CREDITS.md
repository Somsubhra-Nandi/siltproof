# Photo credits and sources

The 40 photographs in the prepared investigation (Bill B1, `frontend/public/data/demo`)
are **illustrative**. None was taken at these drains, and before and after in a pair
show different places. They stand in for the contractor's photos so the case reads
like a real file; the evidence records around them (GPS, time, verdicts) are the
prepared investigation's own and are unchanged.

- **Licences:** CC0 1.0 (Wikimedia Commons) and the Pexels License only. Neither
  requires attribution, so the site shows none; the credits are kept here.
- **Processing:** centre-cropped to 4:3, 1200 x 900, JPEG quality 85, **no EXIF**
  (no camera, GPS or time tags). Three were cropped further before that (noted below).
- **Drain 14** `after-02` is the same file as drain 9 `after-01`; `after-03` is a
  cropped, brightened copy of it. R3 measures 0 and 10 bits (limit 12).
- **Checked:** each source page, author and licence on 10 Oct 2026; faces, number
  plates and branding reviewed at output size.
- Source of truth: `data/photo_sources.csv`. Applied with
  `python data/import_photos.py --sources data/photo_sources.csv --photos-dir <originals> --snapshot frontend/public/data/demo`.
  The originals are kept outside the repository.
